## GEH acceptance (the honest headline)

**GEH<5 at 51.8% of 421 counted approach links** (2 h mean-hourly flows) — **below** the 85% DMRB-style
target. Distribution: GEH<5: **218**, 5–10: **82**, 10–20: **74**, ≥20: **47**.

| iteration | recipe | GEH<5 share |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 15-min counts, direct candidates (-p 0.5, fringe 10), ttt 300 | **51.8%** |
| 1 | denser/windier candidates (-p 0.35, fringe 5, --intermediate 1) | 35.1% |
| 2 | hourly count aggregation + ttt 600 | 42.5% |
| 3 (ACCEPTED) | revert to iteration-0 recipe — deterministic re-build | **51.8%** |

**Why 85% is structurally out of reach on this network** (recorded in provenance as the verdict):
the corridor net is bbox-clipped (boundary intersections lost legs — e.g. Markham/Kingston is a dead-end
stub), 15% of loaded vehicles never insert (avg depart delay 131 s), ~9% teleport under saturation, and
signals run netconvert defaults, not the city's coordinated plans. The modeled network cannot carry the
full counted volumes. **Read calibrated absolute volumes as approximate; baseline-vs-scenario comparisons
remain like-for-like.** Bbox expansion and signal-plan import are the V2.1c-scale levers.

**Recommendation (adopted): `synthetic_demo` stays the interactive edit-mode default; `calibrated_am_peak`
is a batch/offline capability.** The full-length recorded pair is impractical on this hardware; the
bounded peak-hour smoke (`NADI_MAX_T_OVERRIDE=3600`) is the supported way to exercise the calibrated
path end-to-end (truncation visible in `meta.sim_end`, labeled in the run description).

## Run-time hardening shipped along the way (all regression-tested)

1. Zero-movement segments never index/PET-test (waiting queues once caused ~10¹⁰ tests).
2. Teleporting entities (INVALID position sentinel; 3-tuple results) are skipped — one such point once
   rasterized a 2-billion-metre "segment" (the deterministic 4-minute freeze across four attempts).
3. Teleport flush-then-reappear counts a traveler once.
4. TraCI subscriptions (one batch/step) + offline geo-conversion at flush — the per-entity polling loop
   would have cost ~194M socket round-trips per run.
